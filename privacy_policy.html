Hosting

Our host collects the following data transmitted by your browser in log files:

IP address, the address of the previously visited website (referrer request header), date and time of the request, time zone difference to Greenwich Mean Time, content of the request, HTTP status code, amount of data transferred, website from which the request comes, and information about the browser and operating system.

This is necessary to display our website and to ensure its stability and security. This corresponds to our legitimate interest within the meaning of Art. 6 para. 1 sentence 1 lit. f GDPR.

No tracking takes place and we do not have direct access to this data.

Legal Notes on Hosting

As a general rule, a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) must be concluded with the host. However, the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, LDA) has made an exception for the hosting of purely static websites. Where a website serves self-presentation purposes, e.g. of associations or small businesses, no personal data flows to the operator and no tracking takes place, there is no commissioned data processing. The LDA states: „The fact that IP addresses, i.e. personal data, must inevitably be processed even when hosting static websites does not lead to the assumption of commissioned data processing. That would not be appropriate. The (short-term) storage of IP addresses is rather to be attributed to the telecommunications access mediation of the website host under the TKG [German Telecommunications Act] and primarily serves the security purposes of the host.“ (LDA FAQ: Hosting without commissioned data processing, in German) We assume that this exception applies to GitHub Pages.
