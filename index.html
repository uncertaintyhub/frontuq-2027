Frontiers of Uncertainty Quantification:
UQ in Geosciences and Geoengineering


September 14 - 17, 2027, Freiberg (Saxony), Germany

The 6th edition of the FrontUQ workshop of the GAMM Acitivity Group on Uncertainty Quantification will take place September 14–17, 2027, at TU Bergakademie Freiberg, Germany, and will be devoted to the topic “Uncertainty Quantification in Geosciences and Geoengineering”.

From the deep subsurface to the atmosphere, geoscientific models describe complex, multiscale processes that can only be observed sparsely and indirectly. Uncertainty is therefore an inherent part of every prediction, be it of groundwater flow, seismic activity, ocean dynamics, weather, or climate. Challenges such as the energy transition, the sustainable use of natural resources, and the assessment of natural hazards and climate risks make reliable uncertainty quantification more relevant than ever. The workshop aims to strengthen the exchange between the UQ community and researchers and practitioners in the geosciences and geoengineering, bringing together participants from both academia and industry.

We therefore welcome contributions on the development of mathematical and computational UQ methods as well as on their application to problems in the geosciences and geoengineering. Topics of particular interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Bayesian inverse problems and data assimilation
  • Probabilistic forecasting and ensemble methods
  • Uncertainty propagation in coupled multiphysics processes and multiscale models
  • Random field modeling and geostatistics
  • Surrogate models, model reduction, and machine learning
  • Optimal experimental design and value of information
  • Risk assessment and decision-making under uncertainty
